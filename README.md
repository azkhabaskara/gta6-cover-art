Neon Shore Cover Studio — an offline web app that turns any photo and caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art. Six poster layouts, six Vice City sunset / neon colour grades, bold Anton lettering with a Pacifico script subtitle and a badge ribbon, print effects (grain, vignette, halftone, sun rays, frame), per-panel crop and position, undo/redo, and one-click PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Your photos never leave your machine: everything runs from static files, with no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Pick a layout, a grade, and two photos (upload your own or use the samples), write your title, then Export PNG — or Export all 3 sizes at once.
